First Thought privacy policy

Effective: 16 November 2026

First Thought is an alarm and affirmation app for iPhone. This policy says what it does with your information. In short: it keeps everything on your phone, and we never receive it.

What stays on your phone

First Thought stores the following on your device only:

  • The name, goal and answers you give when you set the app up.
  • Your alarms and their settings.
  • Affirmations you write, the ones you mark as favourites, and the categories you choose.
  • Your chosen theme and, if you pick one, a photo you select as a background.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We run no servers for the app, and it has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Permissions

  • Alarms: so your alarm can ring when the phone is locked or on silent.
  • Notifications: so the app can remind you to finish your affirmations if you leave halfway.
  • Photos (add only): so the app can save an affirmation card you choose to share. It does not read or look at the photos you already have. A background photo is chosen through the system photo picker, which gives the app only the picture you pick.

You can change any of these in the iOS Settings app.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We don't see your payment details. Apple tells the app whether you have First Thought Pro so it can unlock it.

Sharing

If you share an affirmation card, you choose where it goes (Messages, Photos or another app). What you share is then in that app's hands, under its own policy.

Children

First Thought doesn't collect anything from anyone, including children.

Deleting your information

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your phone. There is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new effective date.
