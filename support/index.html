First Thought support

First Thought is an alarm that wakes you with a tone, then asks you to tap through a few affirmations and type one before it switches off. If something isn't working, the answers below cover the usual causes. If yours isn't here, write to us.

Contact: support@getfirstthought.com. Please say which iPhone and which version of iOS you have, and what you expected to happen. We read every message.

My alarm didn't ring

Work down this list. The first two cause almost every missed alarm.

  1. Check that alarms are allowed. Open the iOS Settings app, choose First Thought, and make sure Alarms is on. If they are off, First Thought shows an amber notice under the next-alarm time on Home that says so. Tapping the notice takes you to Settings.
  2. Check the alarm is switched on. Open the alarm list from the time on Home. An alarm with its switch off won't ring. A one-time alarm switches itself off after it has rung, as the Clock app does.
  3. Open the app once. Each time First Thought opens, it checks that every alarm you have switched on is scheduled with iOS, and sets any that are missing again. This matters after an iOS update, after restoring a phone from a backup, or if iOS ever clears the alarms.
  4. If it rang and you didn't hear it, try a different tone and check the phone's volume isn't at its lowest. The alarm sounds even when the phone is on silent.

If an alarm still failed, please write to us with the time you set and what happened. The app keeps a short record of what each alarm did. To send it, press and hold the greeting at the top of Home (for example "Good morning") for about two seconds, tap Copy, and paste it into your email.

Why does the alarm ask me to tap and type?

That is the idea of the app. When the alarm rings and you tap Stop, First Thought opens into a few affirmations. You tap through them, one at a time, and then type one. Only then is the alarm finished. It is just enough to bring you properly awake.

How many? Each alarm has a wake-up level. Gentle is 3 taps and 1 typed sentence, Standard is 4 and 1, and Wide awake is 5 and 2. All three are free. Pro adds Custom, where you choose the numbers yourself, from none at all (a plain alarm) up to 8 taps and 5 typed. Set a level when you make or edit an alarm, and set what new alarms start with in About You (the person icon on Home).

Typing is forgiving. Capital letters, punctuation, spaces and accents don't matter, only the letters and numbers. "i am enough" matches "I am enough."

What if I dismiss the alarm and don't finish?

It comes back. If you swipe the app away or dismiss the alarm before finishing the affirmations, it rings again after your snooze time, several times over, until you finish. If you leave halfway, a notification asks you to finish. With snooze switched off, the alarm doesn't come back.

My alarm doesn't turn off with Stop

Tap Stop on the alarm, then First Thought opens and asks for the affirmations. That is how it is meant to work. If Stop does nothing at all and nothing opens, please write to us straight away.

Notifications

First Thought sends one kind of notification: a nudge to finish your affirmations if you leave halfway. Your alarm rings whether or not notifications are on. Some Focus modes, such as Sleep, can hide the nudge. You can turn notifications on or off in the iOS Settings app, under First Thought.

Free and Pro

The alarm is never locked behind a payment and is never made less reliable.

Free: every alarm feature, the three named wake-up levels, two categories of affirmations (100 sentences), two alarm tones, the Gradient background, up to 3 favourites, widgets and sharing.

Pro: all 11 categories (550 sentences), all 12 tones, every background including your own photo, your own affirmations (up to 100), a different focus for each alarm, the custom wake-up challenge, and unlimited favourites.

There is a free trial of 7 days on the yearly plan. You can choose the yearly plan or a one-time lifetime purchase. Nothing you have made is ever taken away if you stop paying: your own affirmations, favourites, and the tones and backgrounds you chose stay, and anything saved in an alarm keeps working.

Restore a purchase

On a new phone, or after reinstalling: open About You, tap the link under your categories that says what the others include, and choose "Restore a previous purchase". You need to be signed in to the Apple Account you bought with.

Cancel the yearly plan

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open the iOS Settings app, tap your name, choose Subscriptions, then First Thought Pro, and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews to avoid being charged again.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Widgets

Add a widget by pressing and holding the Home Screen or Lock Screen and tapping the plus button. Choose a focus category in Edit Widget. Tap a widget to see the next affirmation; it doesn't need to open the app.

My information

Everything First Thought stores, such as your name, goal, alarms, affirmations and favourites, stays on your phone. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

New phone: First Thought doesn't sync between devices. A normal iPhone backup, or moving to a new iPhone with Quick Start, brings your alarms and settings with it. Open the app once afterwards so your alarms are scheduled again.

Delete your information: delete the app. That removes everything it stored on your phone, and there is nothing held anywhere else.
