First Thought

An alarm that wakes you with a tone, then asks for a few kind words before it lets you go.

To switch the alarm off you tap through a short run of affirmations and type one yourself, so the first thing you read each day is something kind. It works on a locked or silent phone, and the alarm itself is never locked behind a payment.

Everything stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
